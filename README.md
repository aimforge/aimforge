## What is AimForge

Not another meaningless target-clicking trainer. This is a complete professional training system that treats aim as a measurable science:

- **Every single shot you fire is recorded** — offset vector, overshoot direction, correction chain, reaction-time distribution
- **AimTop**, a vertical LLM post-trained on shooting data, reads every piece of your data, writes your next training prescription, and auto-tunes your training parameters for you
- The AI doesn't stop at reports — **it builds a model of you**, so your training keeps getting more scientific and your direction more accurate
- A closed loop of Training → Diagnosis → Review, across Web and mobile

## What Gets Measured About You

| | |
|:---:|---|
| **Every shot** | Offset, overshoot direction, corrections until hit — per-shot, not a blended score |
| **Full distribution** | Your reaction time as a whole distribution, not an average |
| **8 skills** | Positioning, reaction, tracking, decision... scored 0-100 each — which layer is weak, one screen tells |
| **Milliseconds** | How fast your tracking locks on, how much your hand shakes — measurable |
| **Hundreds of metrics** | Upload one demo, know frame-by-frame where each duel was lost |
| **Week over week** | Skill trends — what improved this week, what slipped |

## Core Technology

**Real math under every score.** No score here is a gut feeling — there's higher mathematics underneath, and every bit of it translates to plain language:

- **Tracking feels slow?** An exponential fit measures how many milliseconds your lock-on takes. Which link is slow — stated clearly.
- **Fast but sloppy?** Information theory tells whether your speed and accuracy are still coupled — coupled means you haven't trained them apart yet.
- **"Feels off today"?** The third derivative of your trajectory puts a number on it. No guessing.
- **Tuning sensitivity?** Your perception is modeled on a logarithmic law — because your body is a log machine.

**AI warm-up system.** Warm-up is computed, not templated: a multi-dimension readiness questionnaire, multiple warm-up forms regenerated daily from your current state. The AI holds modeling tools — it writes formulas about you, saves them to your profile with confidence levels, and uses them in every subsequent calculation. Fully transparent.

**AimTop.** A vertical-domain model post-trained on massive shooting telemetry and marksmanship kinetics. Sharper diagnosis, tighter attention. A real model — no shortcuts.

**3D ballistics engine.** Faithful game-feel reproduction, directly securing the transfer from training into real matches.

**Data sovereignty.** De-identified encrypted transmission, numerical metrics only. Never fed into model training. Never sold, traded, or given away.
